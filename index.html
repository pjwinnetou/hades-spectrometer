HADES Spectrometer

The six-sector forward spectrometer, to scale (metres): target → RICH → MDC I/II → toroidal magnet → MDC III/IV → TOF/RPC wall. Polar acceptance 18°–85°. Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom.
